Interview prep — Expedia PM, Autonomous Operations
Tell me about a time you reduced manual operations through automation
Behavioral
Answer with the ML-assisted pipeline: 3M+ records, days to hours, 60% rework reduction. Lead with the business problem, not the technical solution. Structure as STAR. End with the metric. Do not start with "I built..."
How do you define success metrics for an AI system?
Product sense
Answer with your routing engine telemetry. Distinguish leading indicators (confidence score distribution, fallback rate) from lagging indicators (match success rate, API cost). Separate health metrics from outcome metrics. This signals PM maturity.
Design a self-healing system for Expedia's search infrastructure
Product sense
Framework: define failure modes → instrument telemetry → build confidence thresholds → automate fallbacks → create escalation paths. You built this. Walk through your routing engine design, then apply it to hotel search and partner APIs. Reference your Expedia demo.
You're from a startup — how would you operate at Expedia's scale?
Pushback
Don't be defensive. "The patterns I designed were built to be scalable from day one because the data volumes forced that thinking. What I'd add is process rigor and stakeholder alignment frameworks that large orgs have codified. Fast learner in structured environments."
How do you prioritize a backlog for an AI operations platform?
Product sense
Talk through your telemetry-driven roadmap: signal patterns from 300K+ monthly API calls surfaced which fallback strategies were triggering most, which drove what to fix. Connect to their JD: "data-driven insights from monitoring, telemetry, incident patterns to prioritize backlog."
